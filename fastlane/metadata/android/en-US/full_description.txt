<i>FastLyrics</i> is an app that downloads lyrics for the song, you're listening too. It is mostly a clone of <a href="https://github.com/QuickLyric/QuickLyric">QuickLyric</a>, but more modern and in active development.

<b>Features:</b>

* Getting the song, currently playing on the device
* Fetching lyrics for a playing song
* Saving lyrics for offline use
* Material 1 and 2 Design

More to come.
